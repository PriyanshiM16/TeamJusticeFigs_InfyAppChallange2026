About Our Project

Your Town is an interactive game where players make money choices to help their town solve big problems like expensive food, housing issues, and sharing.
"Toullie" the rat guide, remembers every choice and teaches players how to be fair, smart, and helpful.
there will also be a kid-friendly version for a younger audience

We created this project for the Infy App Challenge to help show how digital apps and games can improve everyday life and encourage community engagement.
