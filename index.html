About Our Project

Your Town is an interactive game where players make money choices to help their town solve big problems like expensive food, housing issues, and sharing.
"Pizza Rat," the rat guide remembers every choice and teaches players how to be fair, smart, and helpful. Assisting them in challenges, providing helpful knowledge and hints
There will also be a kid-friendly version for a younger audience.

We created this project for the Infy App Challenge to demonstrate how digital apps and games can enhance everyday life and foster community engagement.
